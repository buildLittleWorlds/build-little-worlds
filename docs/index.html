Deployment is publication plus environment: the act of moving a made thing from a private or local condition into a public, addressable, technically maintained condition.

A deployed work is not only a file. It is a host, a URL, a build path, a domain relation, a failure mode, and a future maintenance obligation. To deploy is to accept that making‑public now includes infrastructure.
